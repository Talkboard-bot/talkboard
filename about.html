Talk Board: a free talk board for children who are learning to communicate

Talk Board is a free picture talk board, also called an AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) board. It is made for autistic children and children who are semi-verbal or non-speaking. The child taps a picture and the phone, tablet or computer says the word aloud. Tapping several pictures builds a short sentence, such as "I want drink" or "more play".

Open Talk Board

What it does

  • About 500 words with clear pictures, covering core words (I, want, more, stop, help) and topics like food, feelings, people, places, school and play.
  • Speaks in a calm, gentle voice. The child hears each word as they tap it, and a bigger picture pops up for a moment.
  • Always-there buttons for Stop, Break, Help, Too loud and Don't like.
  • Parents, carers and teachers can add their own words and photos, and practise modelling (pointing to words while they talk).
  • A "how I communicate" passport to share with new carers or teachers.

Free, private and works offline

There is nothing to pay, no adverts and no sign-in. Everything the child or adult adds stays on that device. After the first visit it works without internet. On a phone or tablet you can add it to the home screen so it opens like an app.

How to start

  1. Open Talk Board in any web browser: Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Follow the short "How to use" tour.
  3. Turn the volume up. On iPhone, make sure the silent switch is off.
